JOURNEY JOURNAL / ENTRY 001

THE STORY
STARTS HERE.

NOVEMBER 2026 · MATT & WARREN

We’re Matt and Warren. We share a vision of racing Dakar in 2030, and we’re leading the effort together. We want to build a community that can help turn that vision into a prepared car, a capable team and a place on the start line.

We’re at the beginning. We haven’t settled on a vehicle or a category, and there is no confirmed entry. The work ahead is to understand what’s required, build our skills and bring together people who can help make it possible.

Endurance starts with the preparation. We’ll share the training, decisions, setbacks and progress honestly. We’ll also explore where AI and robotics could help with the car, and where security and automation could improve event logistics. These are ideas to investigate, with any race use subject to the rules.

We’ll meet once a month, in person, in Vancouver, Canada. You can contribute from elsewhere too; you don’t have to attend the race to belong. Motorsport, mechanical work, AI, logistics, media, introductions and ideas all have a place.

We’re also inviting sponsors to help with funding, tyres, equipment, software or services. Let’s build partnerships that show what your support makes possible and give your organisation a meaningful role in the journey.

One shared vision. A community behind it.
Let’s see how far we can take it.

JOURNEY JOURNAL / ENTRY 001

THE STORY
STARTS HERE.

NOVEMBER 2026 · MATT & WARREN

Racing the Dakar Rally has been a dream of Matt’s. Now, with his good friend Warren helping lead the effort, we’re giving that dream a target: 2030.

We’re at the beginning. We haven’t settled on a vehicle or a category, and there is no confirmed entry. The work ahead is to understand what’s required, build our skills and bring together people who can help make it possible.

That preparation is worth sharing. The decisions, training, things that go well and things that don’t. We want to document the journey honestly as it unfolds.

If you have experience in motorsport, mechanical work, logistics, media or technology, or simply know someone we should meet, we’d love to start a conversation. The same goes for potential partners who want to help build this from the beginning.

Two friends. One extraordinary ambition.
Let’s see how far we can take it.
